# Lintel 1.0.0-rc.1: UX improvement proposals (need review)

These are proposals, not defects. None should be implemented without approval from the product or design owner. Each one is the smallest change that addresses the observation.

| ID     | Proposal                                                                                           | Why                                                        | Smallest change                                                               | Risk                                                                  | Confidence |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| UXI-01 | Offer "Add to main menu" when a page is first published                                            | Hand-made pages never reach the site navigation (SPEC-002) | A checkbox in the publish confirmation, on by default for top-level pages     | Menus gain items users didn't expect; keep it unticked for deep pages | Medium     |
| UXI-02 | Warn when publishing a page whose parent is a draft                                                | Live children under a draft parent (SPEC-001)              | An inline note in the Publish menu: "Home isn't published yet. Publish both?" | Low                                                                   | Medium     |
| UXI-03 | Agree a content-width rule: narrow (about 720px) for forms and settings, full for tables and lists | Widths currently vary by screen (VR-10)                    | A design decision first; no code until agreed                                 | Touches many pages                                                    | Low        |
| UXI-04 | On a brand-new site, collapse empty dashboard widgets into one "Getting started" row               | Four large empty cards (VR-11)                             | Hide empty widgets when all are empty; keep "Edit dashboard"                  | Hides information users may want                                      | Low        |
| UXI-05 | Show the update check's technical reason behind "Details"                                          | Raw "(The update feed answered 403)" in user copy          | Move the reason into a disclosure                                             | Low                                                                   | High       |
| UXI-06 | Make the checkout country a select of the market's countries                                       | Free-text codes fail silently (UX-003)                     | A `<select>` built from the cart's market countries                           | Low                                                                   | High       |
| UXI-07 | Link from Orders to "Orders before the move" when archived orders exist                            | Imported history is hard to find (DATA-002)                | A one-line note under the Orders heading                                      | Low                                                                   | High       |
| UXI-08 | Pick one name: "Shop" or "Commerce"                                                                | UX-002                                                     | Change the `t()` strings only                                                 | Docs and screenshots also need updating                               | High       |
