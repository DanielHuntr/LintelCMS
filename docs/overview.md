# Lintel at a glance

A one-page summary for business owners, marketing teams and anyone who isn't a developer.
For the technical detail, read the [whitepaper](https://github.com/DanielHuntr/lintelcms/blob/main/docs/whitepaper.md).

## What Lintel is

Lintel is software for running a website. You use it to write pages and blog posts, add
photos, organise menus and publish changes, all from a clean, simple control panel in
your browser. In time it will also let you design pages visually, add tasteful animation
and run an online shop, all in the same place.

It is **self-hosted**: it runs on a server you or your web agency choose, so your content
and your customers' details stay under your control, not on someone else's platform.

## Who it is for

- **Business owners and editors** who want to update their own site without calling a
  developer for every change.
- **Web agencies** who build and look after sites for clients, and want one system they
  can trust, brand as their own and extend.

## What you can do today

The Lintel 1.0 release candidate is available now. With it you can:

- **Set it up in your browser.** A short guided setup asks what you're building (a
  business site, blog, shop, portfolio or something else) and switches on only what you
  need.
- **Write and publish.** Pages and posts save automatically as drafts. Nothing appears on
  your site until you press **Publish**, and you can schedule a page to go live later.
- **See before you publish.** Live preview shows your draft on the real website, side by
  side with the editor.
- **Go back in time.** History keeps earlier versions, and any of them can be restored.
- **Manage photos and files.** Upload once, describe each picture for people who can't see
  it, and mark the important part so it's never cropped out.
- **Publish in more than one language.** Translate a page and publish each language when
  it's ready.
- **Be found in search.** Set how each page looks in search results and social media, and
  keep old links working with redirects.
- **Connect other tools.** Lintel can tell other services when you publish, and developers
  can build your website with whatever technology they prefer.
- **Have a website straight away.** Lintel serves your site itself, with ready-made,
  accessible templates and themes, or works with a site a developer builds.
- **Design pages visually.** Arrange sections, text, pictures and buttons in the page
  builder and see the page as you build it.
- **Work as a team.** Invite colleagues with roles such as editor or author, and protect
  accounts with two-step sign-in or passkeys.
- **Automate the routine.** "When this happens, do that", including approval before
  publishing, set up without code.

## Is it safe?

Security is designed in from the start. Passwords are stored in a form that can't be
read back, drafts never appear on your public site, uploads are checked so that disguised
programs and unsafe images are refused, and every action checks that the person is allowed to do it.

For the online shop, card details will be typed only into Stripe's secure payment page or
form. They will never pass through Lintel or your server, which keeps your shop's card
security paperwork as light as it can be.

Lintel hasn't had an independent security audit yet; one is planned before version 1.0.

## Cost and licence

The plan is for the main product to be free to use, including for agencies building
client sites, with the code open for anyone to read. A paid **Pro** edition is planned
for agency features such as branding. The licence hasn't been published yet, so these
terms are not final.

## In one sentence

Lintel aims to give you the ease of a hosted website builder, the structure of a
professional content system and a built-in shop, on a server you control.
