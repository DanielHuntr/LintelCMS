# 1. Record big decisions

Date: 2026-10-31

## Decision

Every decision that would be expensive to reverse gets a short record in this folder:
what we decided, why, and what we turned down.

## Why

The team is two people today. Records mean a third person, or either of us in a year,
can see why the code is the way it is without asking.

## Format

Title, date, Decision, Why, Alternatives considered. Keep each under a page.
