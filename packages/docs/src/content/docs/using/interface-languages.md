The interface language is the language of the admin itself: the sidebar, buttons and
messages. It doesn't change your content. To publish your site in more than one language,
add languages in **Settings → Languages** instead.

## Choosing your own

Open **Settings**, then **Interface language**, and pick from **Your language**. The admin
switches straight away, only for you, and remembers your choice on every computer you sign
in on. Choose **Same as the site** to follow the site's default again.

## The site's default

People who manage settings also see **Site default**. It's the language for everyone who
hasn't picked their own, and for the sign-in screen. Whoever set up your server may have
chosen one already.

## Languages that read right to left

In Arabic, Hebrew and other right-to-left languages, the whole admin mirrors: the sidebar
moves to the right, and arrows point the other way.

## Which languages there are

Lintel ships in English. Translations are added by the people who use it; see
[Translating Lintel](/building/translating/) to help.
