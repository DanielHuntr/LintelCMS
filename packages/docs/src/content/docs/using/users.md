## Your account

The account made during setup is a **Super admin**: it can do everything. Your name and
email are shown at the bottom of the sidebar, with a **Sign out** button beside them.

## What people can do

What someone can do in Lintel comes from their **role**. A role is a list of things they're
allowed to do, such as "write entries", "publish entries" or "upload media". Lintel only
shows the buttons and sections a person can use, so an editor who can't publish sees
**Save** instead of **Publish**.

Lintel 0.1 has one role, Super admin. Editor and other roles arrive with the **Users**
screen, which is on the roadmap.

## Adding someone else

Until the Users screen arrives, another administrator is added by whoever installed Lintel.
They stop Lintel and run one command on the computer it runs on (see
[Installing Lintel](/building/install/#more-commands)). The new person then signs in with
their email and the password chosen for them.

## Forgotten password

Ask whoever installed Lintel to reset it. They run one command and type a new password
for you. Resetting signs you out on every device, which is also a good way to sign out a
lost laptop.

## Staying signed in

You stay signed in on each browser until you sign out. If you see **You've been signed
out**, sign in again; whatever you were doing is still on the screen.
