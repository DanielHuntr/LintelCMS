An entry is a **draft** until you publish it. Drafts are only visible in the admin.

## Publishing

Press **Publish** at the top right of the editor. The entry is now live on your site, and
the details panel says **Live on the site since** and the date.

Your website may take up to a minute to show it, because sites keep a copy of what they
read from Lintel for a short while.

## Changing something that's live

Edit the entry as usual. Your changes are saved, but **your site keeps showing the last
published version** until you press **Publish changes**. While they differ, the details
panel says **Unpublished changes**.

So you can take your time: fix a typo today, rewrite a paragraph tomorrow, and publish
when it's all ready.

## Taking an entry off the site

Press **Unpublish** under **Status** in the details panel. The entry becomes a draft
again and disappears from your site. Nothing is deleted.

## Publishing later

1. Under **Status** in the details panel, press **Schedule…** (it's there for drafts;
   unpublish a live entry first if you want to schedule it).
2. Pick the date and time under **Publish on**, and press **Schedule**.

The entry shows **Scheduled** and **Goes live on** that date. Lintel publishes it within
a minute of that time, as long as Lintel is running. If an earlier version was already
live, it stays live until then.

Changed your mind? Press **Reschedule…** or **Cancel schedule** in the same place.

## If you can't publish

Some accounts can write drafts but not publish them. If that's you, the main button says
**Save** instead of **Publish**, and the panel says someone with permission to publish can
make your drafts live.
