The page builder can show a little or a lot. Choose what suits you with **Tools**, at the
top of the builder. The builder remembers your choice on this computer, and you can change it
whenever you like: nothing about your page changes when you do.

| Level         | What you see                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Beginner**  | The essentials: words, pictures, colours, spacing, sections and ready-made animations                   |
| **Designer**  | Everything a Beginner has, plus every style setting, hover and focus styles, and the animation timeline |
| **Developer** | Everything a Designer has, plus the code tools: HTML and CSS, code blocks and pasting HTML as code      |

Everyone starts as a **Beginner**.

## Beginner

**Style** shows a few friendly choices for the selected element: its colours, its size and
spacing, its corners. **Animate** offers the ready-made animations and their sliders. A short
tip points you to **Designer** if you ever want more.

## Designer

**Style** gains a **Simple** and **Advanced** switch. **Advanced** shows every setting:
size, position, borders, shadows and more, and lets you style how something looks while the
pointer is over it or while it has keyboard focus. **Animate** gains **Advanced**, the
timeline.

## Developer

**Developer** needs permission to edit code, which your site's owner gives to roles that
need it. Without it, **Developer** is greyed out and says so. With it, a **Code** panel
appears beside **Style**, **Content** and **Animate**, showing the selected element's HTML
and CSS, and **Insert** offers code blocks. See the building guide for what developers can do.

## The guided tour

The first time you open the builder, a short tour of six steps shows you around: **Insert**
(sections and elements), **Layers**, the page itself (click to select, double-click text to
type), the panel on the right (**Style**, **Content** and **Animate**), the screen sizes and
**History**, and **Publish** with **View page**. Each step dims the rest of the screen so the
part it's about stands out. What the tour says follows your tools: Beginners hear where
**Tools** is; Designers and Developers also hear about **Style for** and, with permission,
**Code**.

Press **Next** to go on, **Back** to look again, or **Skip the tour** (or Escape) to start
straight away. Focus stays in the tour's card while it's open, so it works with the keyboard
and screen readers, and it doesn't move about if your device asks for less motion. The tour
shows once; your browser remembers that you've seen it.

To see it again, press **Help and tips** (the question mark at the top) and choose **Replay
the tour**.

## Tips

Here and there the builder shows a short tip, such as how animations behave for people who
prefer less motion. Press **Got it** to close one. **Help and tips**, then **Show tips
again**, brings them all back.
