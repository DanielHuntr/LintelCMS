Several people can work on a site at once. You can see each other, and you can't overwrite each
other's work.

## Seeing who's here

When someone else is on the same page, their picture or initials appear in the top bar, beside
**Preview**. Press them to see their names and whether they're in the designer or editing content.
In the [content tree](/using/content-tree/), the same small pictures sit on the row of any page
someone has open.

There's nothing to turn on. Each person has their own colour, the same on everyone's screen.

## Editing the same page

In the designer, you see where other people's pointers are and what they have selected, with
their names. Changes appear on your screen as they make them, and yours on theirs. You can both
work on the same page at once: one of you on the heading, say, and the other on the pictures.

- **Undo takes back your own changes only.** Someone else's work stays where it is.
- **Words you type on the page** reach other people when you finish typing.
- **If you both change the same thing,** such as the colour of one heading, the last change stands
  on everyone's screen. If you both change the words of one sentence in different places, both
  changes are kept.
- **Publish** puts the page live with everyone's changes so far. If someone is part way through
  something, talk to them first.

In a page's **Content** view, a field says when someone else is typing in it, and what they save
appears on your screen without losing what you're typing elsewhere.

A client who can only change content can work alongside a designer in the same way. They only change what their designer opened up, wherever they're doing it from.

## If you both changed the same thing

Sometimes both of you change the same thing and Lintel can't tell which should stand. This can
happen when your server doesn't allow live connections, or when you've been offline. Lintel stops
and asks, and tells you who made the other change and what you both changed.

- **Keep mine** saves yours.
- **Use theirs** takes the other person's.

Everything else you each changed stays, either way. The one you don't choose is kept in
[History](/using/history/), so you can bring it back.

## If your connection drops

Keep working. The top bar says **Reconnecting…**, and your changes stay on your screen. When you're
back they're merged with what everyone else did. Don't close the page while it says that: Lintel
will warn you if you try.

If the connection stays down for a while, Lintel saves through the normal route instead, as soon as
it can reach your server.

## On hosting that blocks live connections

Some shared hosting doesn't allow them. Everything still works: you just won't see other people,
and changes appear when pages are opened rather than as they're made. Saves are still checked, so
nobody overwrites anyone else.

## What isn't shared live

- **A/B test versions.** Only the original page is shared live. If someone else saved a version
  while you were editing it, Lintel says so before saving over it.
- **The navigation and footer.** These belong to the whole site, and are saved on their own. If
  someone else changed one, Lintel says so and asks before using yours.
- **Components, menus, global settings, forms and media** save as they always have: the last
  save stands.
