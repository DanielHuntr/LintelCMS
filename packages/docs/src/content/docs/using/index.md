Lintel is where your website's content lives. You write pages and posts in Lintel's
admin, add photos to its media library, and press **Publish** when something is ready.
Your website then shows the published version.

You don't need to know any code to use it. Everything here happens in the browser.

## A few words you'll see

| Word             | What it means                                                                |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Entry**        | One piece of content, such as a page or a blog post.                         |
| **Content type** | A kind of content and the fields it has. "Blog post" is a content type.      |
| **Field**        | One thing you fill in, such as a title, a summary or an image.               |
| **Draft**        | Saved, but only visible in the admin. Visitors can't see it.                 |
| **Published**    | Live on your website.                                                        |
| **Media**        | Photos, logos and documents you've uploaded.                                 |
| **Slug**         | The last part of a page's web address: `about-us` in `example.com/about-us`. |

## Where to start

1. [Set up Lintel](/using/setup/) the first time you open it.
2. [Find your way around](/using/getting-around/) the admin.
3. [Write your first page](/using/writing/), then [publish it](/using/publishing/).
4. [Add photos](/using/media/) and mark what matters in each one.
5. [Start from a template](/using/templates/), then [design your pages](/using/designing-pages/)
   in the page builder.

Stuck? The **?** button at the top right of every admin screen opens the help for that
screen, even without an internet connection. [Troubleshooting](/using/troubleshooting/)
covers the messages you're most likely to see.
