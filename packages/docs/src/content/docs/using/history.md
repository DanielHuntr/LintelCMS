Lintel keeps a version of an entry every time it's saved, published, unpublished or
restored. You'll find them under **History** in the editor's details panel, newest first,
with who made each one. Press **Show all** to see the older ones.

## Going back to an earlier version

1. Find the version in **History**.
2. Press **Restore** beside it, and confirm.

The entry goes back to how it was then. Nothing is lost: the version you replaced stays
in the history, so you can restore it again if you change your mind.

Restoring changes the draft. If the entry is live, press **Publish changes** to put the
restored version on your site.
